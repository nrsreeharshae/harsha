N. R. Sree Harsha

Theoretical and computational physicist specializing in non-equilibrium statistical mechanics, plasma physics, electron-device modeling, and physics-inspired computing.

Email Publications Google Scholar

Profile

Research at the boundary of theory and computation

I develop Generalized Langevin Equation and fluctuation-dissipation frameworks for kinetic-to-fluid coarse-graining, non-Markovian transport, ferroelectric switching, and hardware-aware stochastic optimization. My work combines analytical theory with particle-in-cell simulation and computational benchmarking.

Current appointment: Postdoctoral Associate, Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, University of Rochester.
